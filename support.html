02Frequently asked questions

Downloads & delivery

I paid — where's my download?

Thank you for your order! 🙏 Your download is available instantly in two places: on the confirmation screen right after checkout, and in the confirmation email we send you. If you bought on Etsy, you'll also find it under Purchases and reviews. If the email hasn't arrived in a few minutes, please check your spam/junk folder — that's where it hides most often.

The download won't open, or the file looks damaged.

I'm sorry about that — let's fix it right away. First, try downloading again from your confirmation email or account (a download can cut off if the connection drops mid-transfer), and open it on a computer rather than a phone. If it still won't open, email us at support@blackfuturevision.com with your order number and we'll re-send a fresh file the same day. You will not be left without your art. 💫

Do I really get it instantly? When does it arrive?

Yes — it's a digital download, so there's no shipping. Your file is ready the second your payment goes through, and we email you a copy too. Nothing is mailed physically.

Can I download it again later?

Absolutely. Your download link stays available in your confirmation email and your account, so you can re-download whenever you need it. We recommend saving the original file somewhere safe (a cloud drive is ideal) so you always have it.

File specs & printing

Is this high resolution? What are the exact dimensions?

Yes — these are made for the wall. Each piece is delivered as a high-resolution JPEG, roughly 8000 × 10000 pixels at 300 DPI, in a 4:5 aspect ratio. Some listings also include a PNG or PDF. At 300 DPI it prints crisp and gallery-clean at everyday sizes and scales beautifully up to about 60 inches on the long edge.

Can I print it at 40×60 / 24×36 / 20×30 / 16×20?

Great question. The native 4:5 ratio fits standard frame sizes like 8×10, 16×20, 24×30, and up to 48×60 with no cropping. Sizes with a different shape — like 24×36, 20×30, or 40×60 (those are 2:3) — will need a little cropping, or we're happy to prepare a custom-sized version cut to the exact dimensions you want. Just email us the size and we'll sort it out.

How do I resize it myself?

Easy, and free. Open the file in Photopea (photopea.com — a free in-browser editor):

  1. Go to Image → Image Size.
  2. Turn on "keep proportions" (the little chain icon) so it doesn't stretch.
  3. Set Resolution to 300 and enter your target width or height.
  4. File → Export as → JPG (or PNG) and print.

If the size you want has a different shape than 4:5, crop gently from the edges — or let us make a custom file so nothing important gets cut.

Where should I print it? Can I print on canvas?

You've got three good options:

  • At home — fine for smaller sizes on quality photo paper; set your printer to its best/photo setting and 300 DPI.
  • An online print service — for large, framed, and canvas prints, services like CanvasPop, Fine Art America, or Printful do beautiful work. Upload, pick your size and material, done.
  • A local print shop — bring the file on a USB drive; ask for 300 DPI and matte or luster paper.

For canvas: a gallery-wrapped canvas at 300 DPI looks stunning with these pieces — the deep blacks and gold really come alive on textured canvas.

Licensing & usage

Can I use this commercially — on merch, DTF, products to sell?

Your purchase includes a personal-use license: print it, frame it, hang it, gift it, enjoy it at home or the office. Commercial use — putting the art on products you sell (apparel, DTF transfers, prints for resale, merchandise, branding) — requires a separate commercial license, which we're glad to provide starting at $99. Just reach out with the piece and how you'd like to use it. Full details on our Licensing & Usage page.

Refunds

What's your refund policy on digital downloads?

Because these are instant digital files, all sales are final once the download is delivered — that's standard for digital art. But we will always make it right if something's wrong on our end: if your file is corrupt, won't download, or arrived damaged, contact us and we'll re-send a working file or a replacement, no hassle. See our full Refund Policy.
