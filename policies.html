Licensing & Usage Rights

When you buy a Black Future Vision artwork, you're buying a license to use the file — not the copyright itself. Black Future Vision keeps ownership of the original artwork at all times.

✔ Your personal-use license includes

  • Printing the art for your own home, office, or personal space — as many prints as you like, at any size.
  • Framing it, putting it on canvas, and giving a printed copy as a gift.
  • Personal, non-commercial enjoyment.

✕ Not included in the personal license

  • Selling, sharing, or redistributing the file itself.
  • Putting the art on products you sell — apparel, DTF/sublimation transfers, mugs, prints for resale, or any merchandise.
  • Using it in branding, advertising, or anything you profit from.
  • Claiming the artwork as your own.

💼 Commercial license (available)

Want to use a piece commercially? We offer a commercial license starting at $99, scoped to how you plan to use it. Email support@blackfuturevision.com with the piece and your intended use, and we'll get you set up quickly.

Refund & Return Policy

Digital downloads are non-refundable. Because you receive the full high-resolution file instantly, all sales are final once delivered — there's nothing to physically return, and the file can't be "given back." This is standard practice for digital art, and we appreciate your understanding. 🙏

We will always make it right when something's wrong on our end

If any of these happen, contact us and we'll fix it, fast:

  • Your file won't download or the link is broken.
  • The file is corrupt, damaged, or won't open.
  • You received the wrong file.

How to get help: email support@blackfuturevision.com (or message us on Etsy for Etsy orders) with your order number and a short description. We'll re-send a working file or provide a replacement — usually the same day.

If you have any doubt about specs or sizing before you buy, please ask first — we'd rather help you choose the right piece than leave you unsure after. See our FAQ for file details.

Terms of Service

By purchasing from Black Future Vision, you agree to the following:

  1. What you're buying. A license to use a digital artwork file for the uses described in our Licensing & Usage section — not the copyright or ownership of the original artwork.
  2. Ownership. All artwork, designs, and files remain the intellectual property of Black Future Vision. Copyright stays with us at all times.
  3. Acceptable use. You may use your purchase under the personal-use license included with every order. Commercial use requires a separate commercial license.
  4. No redistribution. You may not share, resell, redistribute, sublicense, or give away the digital file itself. You may print it for your own use as described above.
  5. Delivery. Products are delivered as instant digital downloads. No physical item is shipped.
  6. Support & fixes. If a file is defective or won't download, we'll replace it (see our Refund Policy).
  7. Changes. We may update these terms from time to time. The current version always lives on this page.

Privacy Policy

Black Future Vision respects your privacy. Here's the honest, simple version:

  • What we collect: your email address (to deliver your download and send your receipt) and your order details. Payment information is handled entirely by Payhip, our payment processor — we never see or store your full card number.
  • How we use it: to deliver your artwork, send your confirmation and download link, provide support, and — only if you opt in — send occasional updates about new work. That's it.
  • What we don't do: we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone. Ever.
  • Payments: processed securely by Payhip. Your card details go to them, not to us.
  • Your choices: you can ask us to remove your information, or unsubscribe from emails, any time — just email support@blackfuturevision.com.
